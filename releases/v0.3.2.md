# 101 Downloader 0.3.2

- Stabilize displayed download speed and ETA with a roughly two-second rolling speed average in the table and download details.
- Prevent extra UI refreshes from creating short-interval speed spikes. Samples are at least 250 ms apart and still count progress between samples.
- Clear estimates immediately on pause. Restarted byte counters and long gaps begin a fresh estimate; stalled transfers settle to zero.

Quit 101 Downloader through its tray menu before installing **101-Downloader-Setup-0.3.2-x64.exe**. Existing history, settings, and downloaded files are retained. Reload the browser companion after upgrading.

Verification: 16 UI/sampler tests passed, including bursty progress, extra refreshes, stalls, pauses, counter resets, and long gaps. Clippy passed. The optional native-tray test was skipped. Live UI checks and a full installer lifecycle rerun were not performed. The installer is unsigned.
