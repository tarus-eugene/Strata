Bring your downloads to Strata.

The Chrome companion sends file links straight to your desktop app. Everything travels locally on your computer.

  1. Build the app and bridge

    In your Strata project folder, run:

    .\run.ps1 build

  2. Connect the bridge once

    In the same folder, run:

    .\install-browser.ps1

    This registers Strata for your Windows account. Administrator access is not required.

  3. Open Strata and check the connection

    Open the app, then click the extension icon. Its status should say “Connected to Strata.” Leave Capture downloads enabled for automatic handoff.

Your downloads stay under your control.

Turn capture off to keep downloads in Chrome. You can also right-click a file link and choose Download with Strata, or paste a link into the extension.

If the bridge is unavailable, Chrome resumes its own download. Browser-generated files, private downloads, and downloads flagged by Chrome are left in the browser. Login-only and POST-generated downloads may require Chrome; cookies and passwords are never exported.

To disconnect, run .\install-browser.ps1 -Uninstall and remove the extension in Chrome.
