Bring your downloads to 101 Downloader.

The browser companion sends file links straight to your desktop app. Everything travels locally on your computer.

  1. Install 101 Downloader

    Run the 101 Downloader Windows installer and leave Connect the browser companion selected. If you skipped that option, open 101 Downloader Settings and click Connect browsers.

  2. Load the companion in each browser

    Open your browser's extensions page, enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the extension folder inside your 101 Downloader installation. 101 Downloader Settings has an Extension folder button to find it.

    Chrome: chrome://extensions
    Edge: edge://extensions
    Brave: brave://extensions
    Vivaldi: vivaldi://extensions
    Opera / Opera GX: opera://extensions
    Chromium and other compatible forks: chrome://extensions

  3. Check the connection

    Open 101 Downloader, then click the extension icon. Its status should say Connected to 101 Downloader. Leave Capture downloads enabled for automatic handoff. Load and enable the companion separately in each browser profile.

Your downloads stay under your control.

Turn capture off to keep downloads in your browser. You can also right-click a file link and choose Download with 101 Downloader, or paste a link into the extension.

If the bridge is unavailable, your browser resumes its own download. Browser-generated files, private downloads, and downloads flagged by the browser are left there. Login-only and POST-generated downloads may require staying in the browser; cookies and passwords are never exported.

The companion uses standard Chromium extension and native messaging APIs on Windows. A browser or organization policy that disables unpacked extensions or native messaging can prevent connection.

Building from source? Run .\run.ps1 build, then .\install-browser.ps1 from the project folder. To disconnect, remove the companion from your browser. Uninstalling 101 Downloader disconnects its own local bridge and retains your files and history.
